# Restructura Customer Support Escalation Procedures

Document ID: CS-003
Version: 1.0
Owner: Customer Support
Status: Synthetic demo procedure

## Purpose

This document defines general internal guidance for escalating customer support requests. It is a synthetic demonstration document for Restructura One.

## When to Escalate

A support request may require escalation when:
- The issue cannot be resolved using approved support documentation.
- The issue requires access or authority unavailable to the current support level.
- The request involves a significant service disruption.
- The request requires review by a specialized internal team.
- The documented escalation criteria are otherwise met.

## Escalation Information

Before escalating a request, support personnel should capture:
- Customer issue description.
- Relevant service or product.
- Steps already attempted.
- Available supporting information.
- Applicable ticket or case reference.
- Reason for escalation.

## Escalation Levels

### Level 1: Standard Support

Handles routine customer questions and issues using approved FAQs, service guides, and troubleshooting procedures.

### Level 2: Specialist Support

Handles issues requiring specialized product, technical, operational, or service knowledge.

### Level 3: Management or Specialized Review

Handles significant unresolved issues, service-impacting situations, or matters requiring management or specialized internal review.

## Customer Communication

When an issue is escalated, the customer should receive a clear and professional update where appropriate.

Support representatives should not promise a resolution date, compensation, refund, or other outcome unless it is supported by approved policy and authorized processes.

## AI Limitations

The AI assistant may classify a ticket and draft an escalation recommendation based on retrieved evidence.

It must not independently escalate a ticket, contact a customer, issue compensation, change ticket status, or make an unauthorized service commitment.

## Policy Limitations

This is a synthetic demonstration procedure. Actual escalation decisions should follow the latest approved support procedures and organizational authority requirements.
