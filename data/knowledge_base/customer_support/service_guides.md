# Restructura Customer Service Guide

Document ID: CS-002
Version: 1.0
Owner: Customer Support
Status: Synthetic demo service guide

## Purpose

This guide provides general internal reference information for handling common customer service requests. It is a synthetic demonstration document for Restructura One.

## Service Request Handling

Support representatives should:
- Review the customer's request carefully.
- Identify the service or issue involved.
- Check the relevant approved support documentation.
- Request missing information when necessary.
- Provide a response based on documented information.
- Escalate requests when applicable criteria are met.

## Common Request Categories

Customer requests may include:
- Account access issues.
- Service information requests.
- Technical or operational issues.
- Billing-related questions.
- General product or service questions.

## Response Guidelines

Support responses should be:
- Clear and professional.
- Customer-focused.
- Concise and easy to understand.
- Based on approved support information.
- Free from unsupported promises or commitments.

## Information Verification

Before providing customer-specific information, support representatives should verify the available account or service information through approved systems.

The AI assistant must not invent customer details, account status, pricing, refund decisions, service availability, or resolution timelines.

## Issue Resolution

Where documented troubleshooting guidance is available, support representatives should follow the applicable procedure.

If the available documentation does not address the issue, the request should be escalated or routed according to the applicable support procedure.

## Policy Limitations

This is a synthetic demonstration service guide. Actual customer requests should be handled according to the latest approved service documentation and support procedures.
