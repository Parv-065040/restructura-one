# Restructura Customer Support SOP

Document ID: CS-004
Version: 1.0
Owner: Customer Support
Status: Synthetic demo SOP

## Purpose

This SOP provides general internal guidance for handling customer support tickets at Restructura. It is a synthetic demonstration document for Restructura One.

## Ticket Intake

When a support ticket is received, the support team should:
- Review the customer's request.
- Identify the product, service, or issue involved.
- Capture relevant ticket information.
- Check whether sufficient information is available to classify the request.

## Ticket Classification

Tickets may be classified into categories such as:
- Account access.
- Service information.
- Technical or operational issue.
- Billing-related question.
- General product or service question.
- Escalation-required issue.

Classification should be based on the information provided in the ticket and applicable support documentation.

## Response Drafting

A support response should:
- Address the customer's stated issue.
- Use information supported by approved documentation.
- Clearly explain the next step where applicable.
- Avoid unsupported promises or commitments.
- Maintain a professional and customer-focused tone.

The AI assistant may draft a response, but an authorized support representative should review the draft before it is sent to a customer.

## Escalation Review

A ticket should be considered for escalation when:
- Approved troubleshooting guidance does not resolve the issue.
- Specialized knowledge or authority is required.
- Significant service impact is identified.
- Documented escalation criteria are met.

The reason for escalation should be recorded with the ticket.

## Ticket Closure

Before closing a ticket, support personnel should confirm that:
- The customer's issue has been addressed according to available guidance.
- Required information has been documented.
- Any applicable escalation or follow-up has been completed.
- The ticket record is accurate.

## AI Safety Requirements

The AI assistant must not:
- Send customer communications independently.
- Change ticket status without authorization.
- Issue refunds or compensation.
- Make customer-specific commitments.
- Invent account, pricing, service, or resolution information.
- Escalate or close tickets without authorized human action.

## Policy Limitations

This is a synthetic demonstration SOP. Actual support operations should follow the latest approved Customer Support policies, procedures, and organizational authority requirements.
