## Purpose

This policy provides general information about employee benefits available at Restructura.

## Health Insurance

Eligible full-time employees are covered under the company's standard group health insurance plan.

The standard plan includes employee coverage and may provide dependent coverage according to the applicable plan terms.

Employees should contact Human Resources for enrollment, eligibility, or coverage questions.

## Retirement Benefits

Eligible employees may participate in the Restructura retirement savings program subject to the applicable plan rules.

Participation requirements and contribution details should be confirmed with Human Resources.

## Employee Assistance

Restructura provides access to employee assistance resources for eligible employees.

Employees may contact Human Resources for information about available assistance programs and eligibility requirements.

## Benefits Enrollment

New employees should complete required benefits enrollment during the onboarding period.

Employees are responsible for providing accurate information required for benefits enrollment.

Changes to benefits information should be reported through the approved HR process.

## Policy Limitations

Benefits eligibility, coverage, contribution requirements, and enrollment conditions may vary according to applicable plan documents and employment terms.
