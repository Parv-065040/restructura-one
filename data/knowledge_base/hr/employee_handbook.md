## Purpose

This handbook provides general internal guidance for employees at Restructura. It is a synthetic demonstration document for Restructura One and does not represent a real company's employment policy.

## Working Hours

The standard working schedule is Monday through Friday, with normal business hours from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

Employees should follow the working schedule applicable to their assigned team. Any team-specific scheduling requirements should be confirmed with the employee's manager.

## Attendance

Employees are expected to maintain regular attendance and communicate planned absences to their manager and HR through the approved internal process.

Repeated unexplained absences may require review by the employee's manager and Human Resources.

## Employee Responsibilities

Employees are expected to:

- Follow applicable Restructura policies and procedures.
- Maintain accurate information in required employee records.
- Complete mandatory training within the specified timelines.
- Protect confidential company information.
- Raise workplace concerns through the appropriate internal channels.

## Policy Limitations

This handbook provides general guidance and does not override a specific HR policy, employment agreement, or applicable organizational procedure. Where a specific policy applies, the relevant policy document should be consulted.
