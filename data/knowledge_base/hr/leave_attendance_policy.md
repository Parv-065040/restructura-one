# Restructura Leave and Attendance Policy

Document ID: HR-002
Version: 1.0
Owner: Human Resources
Status: Synthetic demo policy

## Purpose

This policy provides internal guidance on employee leave requests and attendance at Restructura.

## Leave Categories

Employees may request the following types of leave:

- Annual Leave: 18 working days per calendar year.
- Sick Leave: 10 working days per calendar year.
- Personal Leave: 5 working days per calendar year.

Leave balances are subject to the employee's applicable employment terms and HR records.

## Leave Request Process

Employees should submit planned leave requests through the approved internal HR process at least 3 working days before the intended leave date.

The employee's manager is responsible for reviewing planned leave requests before HR records the approved leave.

For unexpected illness or emergencies, employees should notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible.

## Attendance

Employees are expected to maintain regular attendance and follow the working schedule applicable to their team.

Repeated unexplained absences may be escalated to the employee's manager and Human Resources for review.

## Approval

Submitting a leave request does not mean that the leave has been approved.

Leave approval depends on the applicable leave balance, team requirements, and manager review.

The AI assistant must not approve or reject leave requests.

## Policy Limitations

This is a synthetic demonstration policy for Restructura One. Where an employment agreement or another applicable HR policy provides different requirements, the relevant official document should be consulted.
