# Restructura New Employee Onboarding SOP

Document ID: HR-004
Version: 1.0
Owner: Human Resources
Status: Synthetic demo policy

## Purpose

This SOP provides a standardized checklist for onboarding new employees at Restructura.

## Pre-Joining Activities

Before the employee's joining date, HR should:

- Confirm the employee's joining date and role.
- Collect required employee information and documents.
- Prepare the employment and onboarding records.
- Coordinate with the hiring manager regarding the employee's first-day requirements.
- Initiate required benefits enrollment information.

## First-Day Activities

On the employee's first day, HR should:

- Complete the employee's onboarding documentation.
- Provide information about company policies and procedures.
- Introduce the employee to the relevant team and manager.
- Confirm access requirements with the appropriate internal team.
- Explain attendance and leave procedures.
- Provide information about available employee benefits.

## First-Week Activities

During the first week, the employee and relevant teams should:

- Complete mandatory onboarding and compliance training.
- Review the employee handbook and applicable HR policies.
- Confirm that required system and workplace access is available.
- Schedule an initial check-in with the employee's manager.
- Address outstanding onboarding documentation or access requirements.

## Onboarding Completion

HR should verify that required onboarding documentation, training, introductions, and access requirements have been completed.

Any missing requirement should be recorded and followed up with the responsible team or employee.

## Responsibilities

Human Resources coordinates the overall onboarding process.

The hiring manager is responsible for role-specific introductions, expectations, and team integration.

The employee is responsible for providing accurate information and completing assigned onboarding requirements.

## Policy Limitations

This SOP is a synthetic demonstration document for Restructura One.

It provides general onboarding guidance and does not replace role-specific instructions, employment terms, security procedures, or other applicable company policies.
