# Restructura Records Retention SOP

Document ID: LEG-003
Version: 1.0
Owner: Legal and Compliance
Status: Synthetic demo SOP

## Purpose

This SOP provides general internal guidance for retaining and managing business records at Restructura. It is a synthetic demonstration document for Restructura One.

## Record Categories

Records may include:
- Contracts and agreements.
- Compliance documentation.
- Business and operational records.
- Employee-related records where applicable.
- Vendor and third-party documentation.
- Legal review and approval records.

## Retention Principles

Records should be retained according to:
- Applicable company retention schedules.
- Relevant contractual requirements.
- Applicable legal or regulatory requirements.
- Approved departmental procedures.

The applicable retention period should be confirmed from the latest approved retention schedule before a record is deleted.

## Record Storage

Business records should be stored in approved company systems and locations.

Records should remain accessible to authorized personnel for the duration of the applicable retention period.

Sensitive or confidential records should be protected according to applicable security and access-control requirements.

## Legal Hold

When a legal hold, investigation, audit, or similar preservation requirement applies, relevant records must not be deleted or altered until the applicable hold or requirement has been formally released.

Questions regarding legal holds should be escalated to authorized Legal or Compliance personnel.

## Record Disposal

Records may be disposed of only when:
- The applicable retention period has expired.
- No legal hold or preservation requirement applies.
- Disposal is permitted under the applicable company procedure.

Disposal should follow approved company processes.

## Responsibilities

Business teams are responsible for maintaining records within approved systems.

Legal and Compliance may provide guidance regarding applicable retention, preservation, or legal review requirements.

The AI assistant must not authorize record deletion or determine that a legal hold has ended.

## Policy Limitations

This is a synthetic demonstration SOP provided for informational purposes only. It is not legal advice and does not replace applicable laws, regulations, legal holds, contracts, or official company retention schedules.
