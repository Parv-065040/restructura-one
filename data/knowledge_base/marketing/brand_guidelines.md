# Restructura Brand Guidelines

Document ID: MKT-001
Version: 1.0
Owner: Marketing
Status: Synthetic demo guideline

## Purpose

These guidelines provide internal reference standards for maintaining a consistent Restructura brand across marketing communications. This is a synthetic demonstration document for Restructura One.

## Brand Voice

Restructura communications should be:
- Clear and professional.
- Confident without being exaggerated.
- Customer-focused and practical.
- Concise and easy to understand.
- Consistent across channels.

Avoid:
- Unsupported claims.
- Excessive jargon.
- Misleading performance statements.
- Aggressive or overly promotional language.

## Messaging Principles

Marketing content should clearly communicate:
- The customer problem being addressed.
- The value provided by the relevant Restructura solution.
- Practical outcomes supported by available evidence.
- A clear and appropriate next step when applicable.

## Content Standards

Marketing materials should:
- Use accurate product and company information.
- Maintain consistent terminology.
- Clearly distinguish factual claims from promotional language.
- Avoid publishing confidential internal information.
- Avoid claims that cannot be supported by approved company material.

## Visual and Brand Consistency

Marketing teams should use approved Restructura logos, templates, typography, imagery, and visual assets where applicable.

Unapproved visual assets should not be presented as official Restructura brand assets.

## Policy Limitations

These are synthetic demo guidelines. They provide general marketing guidance and do not replace an organization's official brand policy or approved campaign instructions.
