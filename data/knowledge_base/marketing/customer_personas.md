## Persona 1: Business Manager

Role:
Business manager responsible for improving team productivity and making operational decisions.

Goals:
- Access relevant business information quickly.
- Reduce time spent searching through internal documents.
- Improve decision-making using reliable information.
- Understand how AI can support business workflows.

Pain Points:
- Information is distributed across multiple documents.
- Manual information retrieval takes time.
- Difficulty identifying the most relevant internal information.

Preferred Messaging:
Emphasize practical business value, faster access to information, and evidence-grounded AI assistance.

## Persona 2: Department Head

Role:
Department leader responsible for team performance, processes, and departmental outcomes.

Goals:
- Give employees faster access to departmental knowledge.
- Maintain consistency in business processes.
- Improve visibility into department-specific information.
- Introduce AI while maintaining human oversight.

Pain Points:
- Employees repeatedly ask similar policy and process questions.
- Knowledge may be difficult to locate.
- AI outputs need to remain grounded in approved information.

Preferred Messaging:
Emphasize department-specific intelligence, knowledge retrieval, responsible AI, and human oversight.

## Persona 3: Enterprise Decision-Maker

Role:
Senior stakeholder evaluating technology solutions for organizational adoption.

Goals:
- Understand business value of enterprise AI.
- Evaluate responsible AI capabilities.
- Assess how AI can integrate into existing workflows.
- Reduce operational friction caused by fragmented knowledge.

Pain Points:
- Concerns about unsupported AI-generated information.
- Need for clear governance and human approval.
- Difficulty evaluating AI solutions using generic demonstrations.

Preferred Messaging:
Emphasize evidence-grounded responses, department isolation, responsible workflows, and controlled human oversight.
