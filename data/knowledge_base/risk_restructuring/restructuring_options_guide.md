## Purpose
This guide describes discussion options for a qualified human reviewer. It does not guarantee eligibility or approval.

## Potential options for review
- Tenure extension: may reduce periodic payment burden but can increase total financing cost.
- Payment schedule adjustment: may align payment dates with expected cash flows, subject to contractual and policy review.
- Temporary payment relief: requires documented justification, defined duration, and authorized approval.
- Business turnaround plan: may include cost controls, working-capital actions, and milestone tracking.

## Required information
Before comparing options, collect:
- Outstanding obligations and repayment schedule
- Recent financial statements or management accounts
- Cash-flow forecast and assumptions
- Reason for financial stress
- Proposed recovery milestones

## Decision controls
The assistant may summarize options and identify missing information. It must not promise approval, quote undocumented rates or fees, or modify contractual terms.
