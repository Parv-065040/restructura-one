## Monitoring indicators
A preliminary monitoring review may consider:
- Missed or delayed payments
- Deteriorating operating cash flow
- Material variance from forecast
- Increasing overdue liabilities
- Incomplete, conflicting, or potentially manipulated records

## Escalation process
1. Record the observed indicator and its source.
2. Identify missing or conflicting evidence.
3. Prepare a concise case summary.
4. Route the case to an authorized risk reviewer.
5. Record the review outcome through approved human-controlled processes.

## AI assistant boundaries
The assistant can draft a summary, checklist, or escalation recommendation. It cannot change risk ratings, contact customers, approve facilities, or execute financial actions.
