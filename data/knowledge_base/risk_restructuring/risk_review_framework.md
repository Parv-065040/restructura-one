# Restructura Risk Review Framework

Document ID: RISK-001
Version: 1.0
Owner: Risk & Restructuring Office
Status: Synthetic demo policy

## Purpose
This framework supports preliminary internal risk reviews. It is not a credit approval, legal opinion, or binding restructuring decision.

## Review dimensions
A preliminary review should consider:
- Cash-flow stability and operating performance
- Debt obligations and repayment schedule
- Liquidity and near-term obligations
- Business continuity and operational risks
- Quality and completeness of supporting documents

## Evidence requirements
Analysts should distinguish verified facts, user-provided assumptions, and missing information. Do not infer missing financial values.

## Escalation
Cases involving suspected fraud, material data inconsistencies, imminent default, or potential legal action must be referred to an authorized human reviewer.

## Limitations
This framework does not define an automated approval threshold. The AI assistant must not approve, reject, or execute a restructuring.
